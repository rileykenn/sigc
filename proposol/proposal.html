Premium Feature

Interactive Events Calendar

No more digging through PDFs or calling the club to find out what is on. Members and visitors can browse the full 2026 programme right on the website, colour coded by competition type with click to expand event details.

Monthly Navigation

Browse through months with a clean, intuitive calendar grid. Events are colour coded by type. AGU comps in green, Veterans in blue, Women's in purple, social events in orange.

Click for Details

Tap any event to see the full details including event name, type, and description. No more searching through printed schedules.

Why it matters: Visitors checking the website before a trip can instantly see what competitions are running. Members stay informed without needing to call the pro shop.

Premium Feature

Live Weather Widget

A real time weather display showing current conditions in Sussex Inlet. Temperature, wind, humidity, and a 5 day forecast. Golfers checking the site before heading out can see at a glance if it is a good day for a round.

Note: The weather display currently shows placeholder data for demonstration. In the live version, this connects to a weather API service for real time Sussex Inlet conditions, updated automatically.

Premium Feature

Smart Contact Section

More than just a phone number. The new contact section features interactive cards. Tap to call, tap to copy the email, tap to copy the address. Plus a full Google Maps embed so visitors can find the club instantly.

Premium Feature

Online Membership Application

A proper multi step application form replacing the old PDF download. New members can apply directly from the website in under two minutes. Personal details, membership type selection with clear pricing, golf info, and a declaration step. All in a clean, guided flow.

Guided Steps

Four clear steps with a progress indicator. Personal details, membership selection, golf info, then review and submit. No confusion, no friction.

Membership Options

Full Member, Veteran, Women's, Junior, and Social memberships. Each displays the annual fee and a short description so applicants know exactly what they are signing up for.

Why it matters: Removing barriers to joining is the fastest way to grow membership. A visitor browsing the site at 9pm can apply on the spot instead of waiting to call during business hours.

Premium Feature

Facebook Feed Integration

Your Facebook posts displayed directly on the website. Comp results, wildlife photos, event announcements. Visitors see fresh, active content without leaving the site, and are prompted to follow the page.

Premium Feature

Email Newsletter Signup

Capture email addresses from visitors and potential members. A prominent signup section encourages people to stay connected. Comp results, event updates, and club news delivered straight to their inbox.

Why it matters: Email is the most effective way to keep members engaged and bring visitors back. A mailing list is a long term asset for the club. Every subscriber is a potential member.

Also Included

Third Party Services

The premium features integrate with several external services for the live site:

  • Weather API (OpenWeatherMap) for real time conditions and forecast
  • Email Service (Mailchimp or Resend) for newsletter subscriber management
  • Google Places API for live reviews from the club's Google listing
  • Google Maps Embed for interactive club location
  • Google Analytics to track visitor numbers and behaviour

Most API services have generous free tiers that will cover the club's usage. No ongoing software subscription costs are expected.
